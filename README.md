# 9V-RGB-LED-Breadboard-Circuit

A breadboard-based hardware project featuring an RGB (Red, Green, Blue) LED powered by a 9V battery and managed with a 470 Ohm current-limiting resistor, designed and simulated in Tinkercad Circuits.

---

## Components Used

* Power Source: 9V Battery
* Semiconductor: RGB LED (Common Cathode or Common Anode configuration)
* Passive Component: 470 Ohm Current-Limiting Resistor
* Prototyping: Breadboard and Jumper Wires

---

## Circuit Calculations and Logic

* Voltage Management: A 9V battery supplies a higher voltage than standard LEDs can handle individually. A 470 Ohm resistor is integrated into the circuit layout to drop excess voltage and protect the internal semiconductor diodes from burning out.
* RGB Functionality: The RGB LED combines red, green, and blue light-emitting diodes into a single package, allowing multiple color pathways depending on the active pin connections.

---

## Wiring and Connections

* Power Supply: Connect the 9V battery positive and negative lines to the appropriate breadboard power rails.
* Resistor Integration: Place the 470 Ohm resistor inline with the active power path to restrict excessive current flow.
* Pin Alignment: Ensure proper placement across the breadboard terminal rows to maintain correct electrical continuity between the power source, the resistor, and the correct pins of the RGB LED.
